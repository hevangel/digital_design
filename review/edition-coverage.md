# Digital Design edition coverage review

This review records the baseline and the work completed from Luna’s Markdown brief. The baseline companion had 11 concept notes, six edition summaries, a change ledger, and 14 SystemVerilog/VHDL example pairs. The audit identified gaps in edition citations, early minimization, memory decoding and correction, historical circuit analysis, and HDL verification.

# Edition 5 — 2013

**M. Morris Mano and Michael D. Ciletti, Digital Design: With an Introduction to the Verilog HDL**, 5th ed., ISBN 9780132774208 (2013).

## Verifiable changes versus 4th edition

The 5th edition's chapter list now has:

1–8. The same broad number-system → Boolean → minimization → combinational → synchronous FSM → registers → memory → RTL progression.
9. Laboratory Experiments with Standard ICs and FPGAs.
10. Standard Graphic Symbols.

The previous 4th-edition standalone **Asynchronous Sequential Logic** and **Digital Integrated Circuits** chapters are **not chapters in the 5th TOC**. That is a verified *TOC-level omission*, not a claim that every mention of hazards, CMOS, and latches was expunged. The later laboratories also have their own revisions, so don't assume experiment numbers match chapter 11 of the 4th.

## Educational trade-off

A modern beginner gets more time for synthesizable combinational/clocked HDL and controller/datapath examples, but risks missing why glitches, metastability, electrical loading, and races matter. The [asynchronous](../notes/09-asynchronous.md) and [logic-family](../notes/10-logic-families.md) supplements preserve these lessons.

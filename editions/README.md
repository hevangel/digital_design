# Edition history (Mano *Digital Design*)

*Digital Design* has six numbered editions. The first edition credits earlier material to Mano's separate *Digital Logic and Computer Design* (1979). Hardcover, international and electronic releases of the sixth edition carry their own dates and ISBNs. [First-edition provenance](SOURCES.md#e1-print) · [Sixth-edition US and Global print records](SOURCES.md#e6-us)

| [1st](01-1984.md) | M. Morris Mano | 1984, ISBN 9780132123334. Supplied international printing ISBN 0-13-212325-8 | Foundations through ASM and asynchronous logic in the supplied contents |

The third and fourth editions keep standalone chapters on asynchronous sequential design and digital ICs. Starting with the fifth, the course uses the [logic-gate treatment and semiconductor appendix](05-2013.md) for basic CMOS and switch modeling. Use the [asynchronous](../notes/09-asynchronous.md) and [logic-family](../notes/10-logic-families.md) companion notes for the historical chapters and the [HDL reading route](../examples/README.md#hdl-reading-route) for the companion's SystemVerilog and VHDL implementations.

The first-edition preface, contents from §9-4 onward and chapter bodies are still needed for a fuller printing-specific comparison. See [source boundaries](01-1984.md#source-boundaries).

## Changes from the first edition

The preface identifies revisions to Chapters 1–7 and 10, replacement problem sets for Chapters 1–7 and three new experiments in Chapter 11. It identifies Chapter 12 as new, with rectangular symbols following ANSI/IEEE Std 91-1984. Use §§12-2–12-3 to interpret a symbol's qualifiers and dependencies before specifying clock edge, reset polarity and input priority in HDL. [Preface, pp. ix–xi](SOURCES.md#e2-preface)

| Topic | Comparison |
| --- | --- |
| Signed binary | The first edition goes from complements to binary codes. The second inserts signed binary at §1-6, p. 14, as reported in its preface. |
| Map sizes | The §3-4 heading changes from five- and six-variable maps to five-variable maps. The second-edition body states the six-variable map size, then works with two planes for five variables. Both editions start their dedicated tabulation sequence at p. 102. |
| Arithmetic and programmable logic | Ch. 5 changes from MSI/LSI to MSI/PLD components. The contents now group arithmetic in §5-2 and decoding/encoding in §5-5. The preface identifies the internal PAL treatment in §5-9, p. 192, as new. |
| Sequential design | Ch. 6's contents end at §6-8. The first edition had a separate §6-9 for design with state equations. The second derives next-state equations and reads them into a state table in §6-4. The preface reports increased emphasis on D flip-flop design examples. |
| Memory | First-edition §§7-7–7-8 become second-edition §§7-7–7-9. The preface reports a RAM rewrite and a new Hamming error-correction section (§7-9, p. 299). |
| ASM and asynchronous design | Ch. 8 retains the same sequence through PLA control. Both editions begin Ch. 9 with introduction, analysis and latch circuits, the last sections visible in the first-edition scan. |
| Electrical characteristics | The preface identifies §10-2, p. 401, and §10-9, p. 430, as additions. |

The preface reports deletions. An itemized list awaits complete first-edition evidence from §9-4 onward, the first-edition preface and additional body comparisons.

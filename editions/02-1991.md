# Edition 2 — 1991

**M. Morris Mano, Digital Design**, 2nd ed., Prentice-Hall, 1991 (ISBN representative: 9780132129374).

## Documented changes from 1984

- **Revised:** publisher description reports **eight chapters substantially revised**.
- **Added:** a dedicated chapter on IEEE/ANSI Standard 91-1984 rectangular logic-graphic symbols.
- **Added/expanded:** new first-seven-chapter problems and new Chapter 11 experiments.
- **Deleted:** publisher says some obsolete topics were deleted, **without an itemized list**. The specific deleted sections are *unverified*; do not fabricate names.

## Learning goals

Translate the physical circuit symbols into logical contracts. E.g. a rectangle annotated with inversion/sensitivity communicates functionality; a name like “flip-flop” is insufficient without clock edge, reset polarity, and priority. Extend the first-edition topics with [laboratory/symbol notes](../notes/11-labs-symbols.md).

## Edition caution

There are 1991, 1995 reprint, and international ISBN metadata differences. They do not imply a distinct numbered edition.

**Evidence:** [1991 publisher description in Google Books](SOURCES.md#e2).

## What to preserve for later-edition readers

- Chapter 9 covers fundamental-mode assumptions, primitive flow tables, races, hazards and stable-state assignments. Implementing these models in an FPGA requires explicit timing and technology assumptions.
- Chapter 10 covers voltage levels, loading, power, propagation, TTL, ECL, MOS, CMOS and transmission gates.
- Chapters 11 and 12 cover labs and symbolic documentation. Experiment 18 in §11.19, p. 553, is the asynchronous circuit lab.

The third-edition body establishes that both standalone chapters preceded this revision. The first-edition scans confirm the asynchronous chapter and identify a Chapter 10 whose detailed scope awaits inspection.

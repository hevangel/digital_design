# Edition 4 — 2007

**M. Morris Mano and Michael D. Ciletti, Digital Design**, 4th ed., ISBN 9780131989245, 2007. Ciletti is a credited coauthor.

## Cataloged curriculum

1. Digital Systems and Binary Numbers
2. Boolean Algebra and Logic Gates
3. Gate-Level Minimization
4. Combinational Logic
5. Synchronous Sequential Logic
6. Registers and Counters
7. Memory and Programmable Logic
8. Design at the Register Transfer Level
9. **Asynchronous Sequential Logic**
10. **Digital Integrated Circuits**
11. **Laboratory Experiments with Standard ICs and FPGAs**
12. **Standard Graphic Symbols**

## What to preserve for later-edition readers

- Chapter 9: fundamental-mode assumptions, primitive flow tables, races/hazards, stable-state assignments. Asynchronous models cannot be converted to a safe FPGA design simply by changing syntax.
- Chapter 10: logic voltage levels, loading, power, propagation, TTL/ECL/MOS/CMOS, transmission gates. These are electrical, not Boolean-only questions.
- Labs and symbolic documentation appear as separate chapters, including work with ICs/FPGAs.
